## Subscription fix

RevenueCat grants the `Basic` entitlement to the monthly and yearly products. The app and server previously checked `image_generation`. Both now recognize `Basic`, retain legacy entitlement compatibility, and accept the exact active subscription products if the entitlement association is missing.

All five paywalls verify account identity before purchase and apply purchase/restore results before dismissal. Older subscription responses cannot overwrite newer paid access. Generation refreshes subscription status before checking preview and paid allowances. Repeated refreshes and restores preserve already consumed paid allowance.
