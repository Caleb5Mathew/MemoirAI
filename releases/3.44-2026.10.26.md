# Memoir 3.44 (2026.10.26)

## Included changes

- Removes the onboarding photo step and generates the first memory illustration without inferring a face or demographic appearance.
- Limits onboarding narration to 60 seconds with a visible decreasing timer and automatic stop.
- Moves the storyteller choice to the recording step so either the user or the named person can share the memory.
- Keeps the existing Meet Alex introduction and book preview flow.
- Defers the welcome sign-in prompt until onboarding is complete and the third subsequent cold launch.
- Hardens Firebase account transitions, guest recovery, RevenueCat identity handling, purchase recovery, and privacy-safe analytics.

## App Store metadata

### What's New

Onboarding is now faster: share a memory in up to 60 seconds, see the time remaining as you record, and continue without uploading a photo. Sign-in is deferred until after onboarding and later app visits. This update also improves subscription recovery, account switching, and reliability.

### Review notes

Memoir lets people record or type memories, organize them into ten chapters, and generate storybooks or longer memoirs. Sign-in is not required during onboarding. After onboarding is completed, the welcome sign-in prompt becomes eligible on the third subsequent cold launch. Microphone permission is requested only when recording is chosen; onboarding no longer requests a photo. The paywall offers monthly and yearly auto-renewable subscriptions. Review the main flow by completing onboarding, opening a chapter, choosing a prompt, and recording or typing a memory. Subscription purchases use Apple's sandbox environment during review.
